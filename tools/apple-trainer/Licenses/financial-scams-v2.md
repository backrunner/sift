Sift modifications: selected an explicitly reviewed English subset, collapsed
whitespace, replaced the binary labels with the existing Sift taxonomy, removed
ambiguous or repetitive examples and historical-source/evaluation overlaps.
Per-row IDs/hashes/reasons are in `signal-real-source-v26-review.json` and the
admission ledger. No translations or synthetic expansions were made.

The publisher describes consented, anonymized financial SMS/chat/email. This
claim is not independently verified. Several records look template-like, contain
literal fake-link descriptions, or resemble adapted historical SMS; no per-row
donor/channel metadata is supplied. Binary scam labels often cover ordinary
service notifications or promotions. Do not auto-import the complete source,
claim recipient-level independence, or describe every accepted row as real SMS.
