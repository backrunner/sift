# Sift Support

Sift is a privacy-first iOS SMS filtering app. For support, bug reports, data
privacy questions, or App Store review follow-up, use the online form:

**[sift.alkinum.com/support](https://sift.alkinum.com/support)**

The form is available in Chinese, English, and Japanese. Leave an email and
your request; no account is needed. App and device information is optional.
Keep the ticket reference shown after submission for follow-up. Please omit
real SMS messages, phone numbers, verification codes, and credentials.

You can also email:

**[support@alkinum.io](mailto:support@alkinum.io?subject=Sift%20Support)**

Include `Sift` in the email subject and briefly describe the issue.

You can also open a public issue after the repository is published:

https://github.com/backrunner/sift/issues

## Data And Privacy Requests

Sift implements export and deletion controls in the app:

- Export contributed samples: Settings -> Data & Privacy -> Export all my
  submissions.
- Erase contributed samples: Settings -> Data & Privacy -> Erase all submitted
  data.
- Withdraw anonymous contribution consent: turn off the anonymous contribution
  toggle in the app.

For anything that cannot be handled in the app, email
[support@alkinum.io](mailto:support@alkinum.io?subject=Sift%20Support) and
include `Sift` in the subject.
